About Me

Hi, I’m Alex Carter — a passionate and results-driven web developer with a strong focus on creating engaging and user-friendly digital experiences. With over 5 years of experience in the industry, I’ve worked on a wide range of projects, including corporate websites, e-commerce platforms, and custom web applications. I specialize in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and WordPress, and I take pride in writing clean, efficient, and scalable code. I approach every project with creativity, attention to detail, and a strong sense of collaboration. Whether I’m working independently or with a team, my goal is always the same: to bring ideas to life through thoughtful design and smart technology. When I’m not coding, I enjoy experimenting with new frameworks, contributing to open-source projects, and staying up-to-date with the latest trends in web development.
